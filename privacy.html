Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

Code Breaker is a code-guessing game. Contact: support@codebreaker.app.

What we collect

  • Game account. A random account ID created when you open the game. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, we also store the email address that the provider shares (Apple may share a private relay address).
  • Display name that you choose.
  • Game data. Trophies, rank, online match results (opponent, number of guesses, time), and settings. Level stars and Daily Code results stay on your device.
  • Purchases. Whether you have Premium and when it renews. Payment details are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card.
  • Reports that you send about another player.

The game shows no ads and does not track you across other apps. We do not collect your contacts, location, photos or microphone.

Why we use it

  • To run online matches, rankings and leaderboards.
  • To give you Premium after a purchase.
  • To stop cheating and abuse.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase (database and sign-in).
  • RevenueCat (subscription status).
  • Apple and Google (sign-in, payments).

Your display name and trophies are visible to other players on the leaderboard and in matches. We do not sell your personal data.

Your choices

  • Delete your account any time: Settings → Delete account. This removes your account, trophies and match history from our servers. Cancel a subscription separately in your App Store or Google Play settings.
  • Ask for a copy of your data: email us.

Children

Code Breaker is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes and change the date above.
