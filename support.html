Support

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Contact us

Email support@codebreaker.app. We answer within 2 working days.

Restore Premium

Settings → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account that bought Premium.

Cancel Premium

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Keep your progress

Settings → Save my progress, then sign in with Apple or Google. Without sign-in, progress is lost if you delete the app.

Delete your account

Settings → Delete account.
